This cartoon by Tom Toro is an absolute classic, and it really keeps growing on you. Indeed, for a teeny, 
tiny moment we had _so much shareholder value_!

![Three children sit around a small campfire in front of a ruined city skyline as a man in a tattered business suit tells them, 'Yes, the planet got destroyed. But for a beautiful moment in time we created a lot of value for shareholders.'](/assets/shareholder-value.png)

The cartoon [appeared in the New Yorker][1], and [Tom Toro said himself in an interview][2] that the image is from 
November 25th 2012.
