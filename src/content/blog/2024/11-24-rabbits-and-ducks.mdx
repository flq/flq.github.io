A couple of years ago I already [shared an image](/posts/careful-mate) that to me conveys a deep truth about the human condition and is one of my all-time favorites.
Here comes another favorite that I would want to keep on my page. I was reminded of it when I was discussing
with my Mum that even though we humans are **genetically** so similar that basically every human being can have sex with some other
human being on this planet a conceive a child, our **cultures** and upbringing can make us so different.

This image also conveys the incredible power of believing in something to the point where simple facts and truths cannot be recognized anymore for what they are.
