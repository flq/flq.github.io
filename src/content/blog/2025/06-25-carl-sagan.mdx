I saw today in a post that **Carl Sagan's Cosmos** [has been made available on archive.org][1].

This show is about 45 years old and left a huge impression on me as a child. The narrator is [Carl Sagan][2],
to me one of our great mentors, an astute observer, a curious person, kind, thoughtful,
someone I deeply admire.

![Carl Sagan explaining the Universe](/assets/carl-sagan.jpg)

The soundtrack contains classical music as well as beautiful songs from Vangelis. The title track of **Cosmos**
is just beautiful and sets the tone of this show.
